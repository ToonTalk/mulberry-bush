Every drawing in the film was traced into outlines and is redrawn here, twelve drawings a second, on paper and grain made by the page. The soundtrack is new and is played live as you watch: Here We Go Round the Mulberry Bush, on the beat of the film’s original recording (music then by Nancy Koch, accordion, and Tommy Magee, fiddle). An accordion keeps the dance going throughout. Each way of drawing a dancer has its own instrument, and it plays only while its dancers are on the screen. Space plays and pauses; the arrow keys step through the drawings. The film itself is on YouTube. How it was made.
