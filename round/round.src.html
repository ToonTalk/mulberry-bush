In 1976 the film was the middle of a live piece in Loops at Harvard’s Carpenter Center: four dancers went round a backlit screen, the film was projected, and the dancers came round again, with Nancy Koch on accordion and Tommy Magee on fiddle in the front row. This version keeps that shape and uses none of the film’s drawings. The film in the middle is a paper wheel of the kind that came before cinema. Every dancer on it is drawn a fraction of a step later than the one beside it, so when the wheel turns behind a shutter the ring dances, and nothing moves except the wheel going round. The tune is Here We Go Round the Mulberry Bush; each way of drawing a dancer brings its own instrument. Space plays and pauses. The 1976 film itself is on YouTube. How it was made.
